# Tuesday 24 November 2026: 38 stories, 8 regions, 12 fields — Huang back as Nvidia soars 31%, COP31 collapses, NATO sends AWACS to Poland

A flat one-page news poster summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 38 stories across 8 regions and 12 fields, drawn fresh with Python/PIL from verified reporting (CNN, Reuters, AP, UN, Amnesty International and others).

Three leads anchor the day: Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO as the shares jump 31% in a day; COP31 in Antalya collapses with no deal — the first major COP breakdown since 2009; and NATO authorises an AWACS deployment to Poland after three Russian cruise missiles landed there.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory. `build-image.py` draws the poster deterministically from the story data embedded in it — no network, no fetched data at build time.
