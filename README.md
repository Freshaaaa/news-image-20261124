# Tuesday 24 November 2026: 38 stories, 8 regions, 12 fields — 96 drones in one night, Nvidia +31.5% lifts Taiex +9.0%, Libya demands answers

A one-page news poster summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 38 stories across 8 regions and 12 fields, drawn fresh with Python/PIL from verified reporting (Reuters, WSJ, AP, BBC, Al Jazeera, Kyiv24, Ukrinform, CNA, Morningstar).

Three leads anchor the day: 96 drones fired over Ukraine in one night; Nvidia jumps 31.5% and lifts Taiex +9.0% on Huang's return; and Libya demands answers over the sunken warship. v3 draws each lead as a full scene — a drone night over Kyiv, the Taipei chip rally, and the Libya naval strike — instead of flat icons.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory. `build-image-v3.py` draws the poster deterministically from the story data embedded in it — no network, no fetched data at build time. v3 (build-image-v3.py) is the live entry: full-scene lead illustrations, sharpened masthead lead, stronger map dots; v2's builder is kept as `build-image.py`.
